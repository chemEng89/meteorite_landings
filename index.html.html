Meteorite Locations

Data: Kaggle. This world map plots the locations of 44,010 meteorites (of 45,716 named specimens) with valid latitude, longitude, and mass records. Each point is colored by meteorite type—Stony, Stony-Iron, Iron, or Other. Stony meteorites make up 98% of the total and are divided into two groups- chondrites and achondrites- based on their physical and chemical properties. Hovering over a point reveals a tooltip with the meteorite’s data, including its recommended class (e.g. H5, howardite, IAB complex, etc.) and its Fall status- whether the specimen “Fell” (observed during descent) or was simply “Found”.

Meteorite Mass Distribution

Data: Kaggle. Stacked histogram of meteorite log₁₀ mass by type. Because the mass span (0.01 g to 60 000 000 g) covers almost ten orders of magnitude, a log₁₀ scale was used. The tooltip shows each bin range, the equivalent mass range using SI-prefix notation, and count by meteorite type. The most massive meteorites (6.8 - 7.8) are type Iron.
